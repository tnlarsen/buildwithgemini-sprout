# Agent Spec: Sprout (Plant Care Assistant)

## Overview
Sprout is a plant-care assistant designed to track plant inventory, maintain watering schedules, issue watering reminders, and diagnose plant health problems using Gemini 2.5 Flash multimodal vision.

## Language
`python`

## Example Use Cases
1. **Plant Registration**: "Add my Monstera Deliciosa in the Living Room; it needs watering every 7 days."
   - *Expected Output*: Calls `add_plant` and confirms the Monstera is registered with a 7-day watering cycle.
2. **Watering & Reminders**: "Which plants need watering today?" / "I just watered my Fiddle Leaf Fig."
   - *Expected Output*: Calls `check_reminders` or `water_plant`, accurately calculating days since last watering.
3. **Multimodal Health Diagnosis**: [Attaches image of yellowing plant leaf] "Why are the leaves on my Peace Lily turning yellow?"
   - *Expected Output*: Analyzes the photo using Gemini vision, identifies likely overwatering/nitrogen deficiency, suggests remedies, and includes a pet toxicity warning for cats/dogs.

## Tools Required
- `add_plant(name: str, species: str, location: str, watering_interval_days: int)`: Adds a plant profile.
- `list_plants()`: Returns all registered plants and their current watering status.
- `water_plant(name: str)`: Records today as the last watered date for a specified plant.
- `check_reminders()`: Identifies plants that are due or overdue for watering.

## Constraints & Safety Rules
- **Pet Safety Warning**: Always alert the user if a plant species is toxic to household pets (cats/dogs).
- **Safe Care Advice**: Recommend safe, non-toxic, organic solutions rather than hazardous agricultural chemicals.
- **Accurate Schedule Math**: Strictly compute watering due dates based on current system time and watering interval.

## Success Criteria
- **Tool Accuracy**: 100% correct tool invocation for inventory and schedule operations.
- **Pet Safety Compliance**: 100% inclusion of pet toxicity warnings for known toxic species.
- **Eval Benchmark**: Achieve a mean response quality score >= 4.0/5.0 on `agents-cli eval run`.
